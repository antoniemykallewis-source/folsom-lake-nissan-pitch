What we found

FINDINGS. Worth fixing.

01

An active ad shows an old deadline.

A Rogue lease ad marked active says delivery was required by June 1, 2026. Shoppers may ask for an offer whose printed deadline has passed. October 26; ad 2061193171422220. Confirm live terms with the ad owner. View source ↗

02

The phone test leaves shoppers waiting.

The largest visible item took 24.2 seconds to appear in the mobile test. A slow first visit can interrupt the path to stock or service. One slow-4G lab test; real-user mobile main-content timing was 1.8 seconds. View source ↗

03

Messages have no clear reply window.

The contact form asks for details but does not say when the team will reply. Shoppers comparing dealers cannot tell how long to wait. Visible form only. No message submitted; delivery and staff response were not tested. View source ↗

How we would help

HERE’S THE FIX. The work we’d do.

01

Match every ad to a live offer.

Review active ads with the sales team. Replace past deadlines, confirm stock and link each offer to matching current terms.

02

Lighten the mobile first visit.

Reduce oversized images and unused code. Load optional tools later, then retest stock and service journeys on slower connections.

03

Tell shoppers what happens next.

Keep the email/phone choice and add a realistic reply window beside Submit. Give urgent shoppers a clear phone route.
